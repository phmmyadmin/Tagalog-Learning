__1\.Personal pronoun__

A pronoun replace a noun in Tagalog sentence to make the sentence less repetitive\. Two important types of  <span style="color:#ff0000">tagalog</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> pronoun </span> that are often used

__personal pronouns __ \( <span style="color:#ff0000">ako\,kami\,tayo\,ikaw\,kayo\,siya</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> and </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">sila</span> \) and

<span style="color:#ff0000">PERSONAL PRONOUNS</span>

The ff\. Pronouns are nominative in form and can only be used as the subject of a verb

in a sentence\.

THE PERSONAL PRONOUN  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __AKO__ </span>  __ __ \(MEANING  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __I__ </span>  _\)_

The pronoun  __ako__  __ __ is the equivalent of the English pronoun / and\, when used as a subject\, can come at the beginning of the sentence before the verb or\, in the inverted word order\, after the predicate\, as in the following examples:

__Ako ay __  __si__  __ John\. 				__  I am John\.  __	__

__Ako ay __  __lalaki__  __\.				 __ I am a man\.

__Masipag__  __ __  __ako__  __\.				__  I am industrious\.  __	__

__Malusog__  __ __  __ako__  __\.				 __ I am healthy\.

<span style="color:#ff0000"> __AKO__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __ __ </span> is linked by  <span style="color:#ff0000">AY</span>  to the predicate in the first two sentences above using the subject\-predicate word order\. Note chat in this type of sentence\, ay may be shortened to  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __'y__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> when the preceding word  <span style="color:#ff0000">ends in a vowel</span> \. The shortened form  <span style="color:#ff0000">'y</span>  is also used after kami \(we including he/she\, and\)\, tayo \(we including you\, he/she\, and /\)\, and other pronouns ending in vowels\.

Ako'y si John\.		I am John\.

Ako'y lalaki\.		I am a man\.

Ako'y nag\-aaral\.		I am studying\.Kami’y nag aaral 		We are studying

<span style="color:#ff0000">2</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">\.THE PERSONAL PRONOUNS </span> KAMI  <span style="color:#ff0000">AND </span> TAYO <span style="color:#ff0000"> \(MEANING WE\)</span>

The pronoun we is expressed by  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __KAMI__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> and  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __TAYO__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> in Tagalog\.

<span style="color:#ff0000">Kam</span> i means we in the sense of  <span style="color:#ff0000">he/she </span> and <span style="color:#ff0000"> I excluding the person spoken to</span> \, whereas Tayo means we in  <span style="color:#ff0000">the sense of you\, he/she\,</span>  and  <span style="color:#ff0000">I\, or you \(the person spoken to\) </span> and I\.

Kami ay sina John at Lisa\. 			 We are John and Lisa\.

Kami'y magkapatid\. 			 	 We are brothers and sisters\.

<span style="color:#ff0000">3\.</span> The personal pronouns  <span style="color:#ff0000">IKAW </span> and <span style="color:#ff0000"> KAYO \(meaning you\)</span>

The pronoun  <span style="color:#ff0000">IKAW </span> is used to express  <span style="color:#ff0000">YOU</span>  when it is used at the  <span style="color:#ff0000">beginning of the sentence</span> \, however in the inverted word order where the subject comes after the predicate\, it is shortened to  <span style="color:#ff0000">ka\.</span>

4\. The personal pronouns  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __siya__ </span>  and  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __sila__ </span>  \(meaning  <span style="color:#ff0000">he/she </span> and  <span style="color:#ff0000">they</span> \)

The pronoun <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __SIYA__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> is singular meaning  <span style="color:#ff0000">he or she</span> \.

Siya ay si Peter\.		He is Peter\.

Siya’y isang bata\.		He is a child\.

Marunong siya\.		He is intelligent\.

Mabait siya\.		She is good\.

The plural form of  <span style="color:#ff0000">SIYA </span> is  <span style="color:#ff0000">SILA </span> meaning  <span style="color:#ff0000">they</span> \.

Sila ay sina John at Mary\.		 They are John and Mary\.

Sila ay magkaibigan\.		 They are friends\.

Marurunong sila\.			They are intelligent\.

In a polite or formal situation\, either one of the plural pronouns  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __kayo__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> and  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __sila__ </span>  may replace the singular pronoun  <span style="color:#ff0000">ikaw</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> or  <span style="color:#ff0000">ka </span> in sentences and questions to  <span style="color:#ff0000">indicate respect\.</span>

Kayo po ba ay guro? 			 Are you a teacher\, sir/madam?

Sila po ba ay guro? 			 Are you a teacher\, sir/madam?

Kayo ay mabait\.				You \(plural\) are kind\.
