The Article  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _SI_ </span>  and  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _SINA_ </span> The article SI is use before the name of a person and the plural article  <span style="color:#ff0000">SINA</span>  is used before the name of two or more persons\. Ex\.  Liza is a woman				 <span style="color:#ff0000">Si</span>  Liza ay babaeSimon and Tom are good\.		 <span style="color:#ff0000">Sina</span>  Simon at Tom ay mababaitHelen and tom are children\.		 <span style="color:#ff0000">Sina</span>  Helen at tom ay mga bata\.

<span style="color:#ff0000">Note that </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">tagalog</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> sentences follow a plurality rule where either the subject or predicate may be plural\. However\, the pluralization of both subject  and predicate is optional\. In the sentence above\, the article </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">sina</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> indicates that the subject is plural while </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">mga</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> \( a plural marker that has no meaning on its own\) before bata indicates that the noun is plural\. </span>

Adjective form their plural by  <span style="color:#ff0000">duplicating the first syllable of the root word</span> \.

For ex\. \(kind root word is  <span style="color:#ff0000">bait\)</span>  	ma bait=singular of good		prefix mababait  ma ganda

The Article of  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _ANG_ </span>  and  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _MGA_ </span> The article  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _ANG _ </span> is used before the name of a single thing or place while  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _ANG MGA_ </span> \(manga\) is used before the names of two or more things or places\.Halimbawa:The child is good\.				Ang bata ay mabaitThe children and the dogs are good\.		 <span style="color:#ff0000">Ang </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">mga</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> bata at  <span style="color:#ff0000">ang </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">mga</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">aso</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> ay mababait\.

The article   <span style="color:#ff0000">ANG</span>

The article   <span style="color:#ff0000">ANG</span>  must always precede the  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __name of a place__ </span> \. However\, the article  <span style="color:#ff0000">MANGA</span>  may be place before the adjective\, which must then take on a plural form if there is more than one name of place in the subject \, or before the noun\. Ex\.

Ang Manila ay isang lungsod ng Pilipinas\.			Manila is a city in the Philippines

_Ang Manila at ang Washington _ D\.C ay mga lungsod 		 _Manila and Washington _ D\.C are cities  <span style="color:#ff0000">Note that it is acceptable to delete the second ANG before the second name of a place and the meaning of the sentence will not change</span>

SENTENCE FORMATION

__There are two basic way to form a sentence in a __  __tagalog__

I\.Subject\-predicate word order

II\.Inverted or transposed sentence order

1\.SUBJECT\-PREDICATE WORD/SENTENCE ORDER

Where the  <span style="color:#ff0000">subject appears </span> at  <span style="color:#ff0000">the beginning of the sentence</span> \.A sentence in this order when the subject is the most important element in the sentence and it comes first within the sentence\. In this order\, the word <span style="color:#ff0000"> AY</span> \(is\) & \(are\) is used\.

Subject\- tell us who or what the sentence is aboutPredicate tells us what the subject  <span style="color:#ff0000"> _does or is \._ </span> It contains the verb and all the words related to action or state\.

Example: Halimbawa

1\. <span style="color:#ff0000">Ang </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">bahay</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> </span> ay Malaki\.			 <span style="color:#ff0000">The house </span> is big\.

2\. Si Peter ay mabait	\.			Peter is good\.

3\. <span style="color:#ff0000">Sina Peter at Mary </span> ay mga bata\.		Peter and Mary are children\.

4\.  <span style="color:#ff0000">Ang </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000">mga</span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> bata </span> ay kumakain\.		 <span style="color:#ff0000">The children </span> are eating\.

5\.Ang babae at lalaki ay nagluluto		The woman and the man are cooking\.

__THE INVERTED OR TRANSPOSED SENTENCE ORDER__

A sentence is  <span style="color:#ff0000">in the inverted or transposed word </span> order  <span style="color:#ff0000">when the predicate comes at the beginning of the sentence before the subject\.</span>  The word  <span style="color:#ff0000">AY </span> is not used the inverted word order sentence\.Example: Halimbawa

Malaki ang bahay			The house is big		Ang bahay ay malaki

Kumakain ang bata			The child is eating

Mabait si Peter			Peter is good/kind

The focus or emphasis in such sentence is on the  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __predicate rather than on the subject__ </span> \.

In inverted word order sentences having  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __plural nouns as their subject__ </span> \, the word  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __mga__ </span> \-not  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __ang __ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __mga__ </span>  <span style="color:#ff0000"> __ __ </span> comes before the first nouns\.

Example: Halimbawa

Mga lungsod ang Manila at Cebu			Manila and Cebu are cities

Mga babae sina Lisa at Cha				Lisa and Cha are women
