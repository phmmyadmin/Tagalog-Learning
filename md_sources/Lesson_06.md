__II\. THE PLURAL FORMS OF QUESTION WORDS__

In Prev\.lesson\, the pluralization of either or both the predicate and subject of a sentence has been pointed out\. Plural markers such as  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __sina__ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __\, __ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __nina__ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __\, ng __ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __mga__ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __ __ </span> and  __ang __  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __mga__ </span>  __ __ have also been introduced\.

Question words are not exempt from the plurality rule either\.

The plural forms of these  <span style="color:#ffff00">interrogatives</span>  are formed by  <span style="color:#ffff00">duplicating the question words or certain syllables of the question words </span> based on the following rules:

I\.	When a word is composed of  <span style="color:#ffff00">two syllables</span> \, the  <span style="color:#ffff00">whole word is duplicated</span> :

alin becomes alin\-alin

sino becomes sinu\-sino

II\. When a word has  <span style="color:#ffff00">three or more syllables</span> \, only the  <span style="color:#ffff00">first two syllables are duplicated:</span>

__kanino__  __ __ becomes  __kani\-kanino__  __	__

__magkano__  __ __ becomes  __magka\-magkano__

III\.The o in the last syllable becomes  __u __ in the first half of the new duplicated word\.

__ano__  __ __ becomes  __anu\-__  __ano__  __	__  __sino__  __ __ becomes  __sinu\-sino__

The plural forms of these interrogative words are used when more than one thing is being referred to and when an answer in the plural form is expected\.

The interrogatives  <span style="color:#ffff00"> _nasaan_ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __ and __ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> _bakit_ </span>  <span style="color:#ffff00"> __ __ </span> are never duplicated for the simple reason that nothing or no one can be in more than one place at the same time nor can there be more than one explanation for an event or action\.
